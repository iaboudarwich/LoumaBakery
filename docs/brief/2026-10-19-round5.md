# Louma Bakery, designer review round 5 (Samaaya, brief deck slides 15 to 21, "Samaaya Edits/Suggestions")

Source: Milla's designer, Samaaya, added review notes to the brief deck (slides 15 to 21). Round 5 is built in two parts: 5A (site-wide: type, header, footer, the Menu and Shop headers) and 5B (the home hero, full-bleed photos, the Our Story layout). Questions for Milla are marked as such; nothing is built for them.

## A. All copy (5A)
1. "Increase kerning (space between letters) on all copy, feels a little tight/harder to read right now: 1-3pts" (with a before/after sample of a body line; the after is about 1% wider). One body letter-spacing token for all body copy: paragraphs, intros, small text, menu items and notes, shop notes and the footer. Headings keep their own tighter tracking; labels and buttons keep theirs.

## B. Photos (5B)
2. "Full bleed images, particularly on the homepage, might help the site feel more bespoke and less template-y in areas". Done in 5B:
   - The home hero goes full-bleed: the current croissant-grid.jpg edge to edge, with the tagline centred over it in Owners Narrow Bold, uppercase, butter, and a soft scrim.
   - The baguette pen loop stays, above the tagline, in butter.
   - Both home photo pairs go edge to edge.

## C. Header and footer (5A)
3. "Increase padding above the Site menu". A taller header bar: clearly more space above the logo and links on computers, a little more on phones.
4. "Remove hairline". The 1px line under the header goes.
5. "Would love a bigger/more prominent illustration in the footer!" The footer face grows from 56px to about 160px wide on computers (about 112px on phones), the wordmark scaled to sit under it. Still static, still butter.
6. "Align type styles on menu and footer (Should be semibold + condensed?)". One condensed style for the header links and the footer: Owners Narrow 500 (Medium, the closest to semibold in the family) for the header links, the footer page links, the "VISIT" label and the copyright. The address, hours and emails stay in Owners 400 body text. This adds a fourth font file, owners-narrow-500.woff2.
7. "Add icons to replace IG/Tiktok for simplicity". Instagram and TikTok become small butter icons, each with its name for screen readers.
8. "Adjust spacing in footer to create more clarity and differentiation between sections of information". She marked three gaps: VISIT and the address, the two hours rows, the two emails. Inside a group the lines sit tight (the label right above its content, the hours as one block, the emails as one block); between groups (address, hours, emails, icons) the gap is about three times larger.

## D. Our Story (5B)
9. "This page might be feeling a little long. Love the letter at the beginning, but feels like it could be combined w/ "Named after our sister". Named after our sister could be the headline on the page, since you will have already clicked into the "Our Story" tab". In 5B: the H1 becomes "Named after our sister", then the letter, then the Named-after paragraphs with no second heading. No text is deleted.
10. "Collage photo could go to the left or right of the text sections, to lessen the amount of scrolling". In 5B: on computers the collage sits in a sticky side column beside the remaining sections.

## E. The Louma Initiative (question for Milla)
11. "Any team or family photos?" A question for Milla. Nothing to build until she sends photos.

## F. Menu (5A)
12. "The Menu title and body copy could be two columns instead of one, to lessen the amount of scrolling". From 64rem: "Menu" on the left, the intro paragraphs on the right, the first paragraph level with the title; stacked below 64rem. One shared split page-header pattern, used by Menu and Shop.
13. "Increase spacing between menu sections and the body copy above it". About twice the current gap between the intro and the row of category links, and a clear gap between that row and the first category. The links row stays full width.

## G. Shop (5A)
14. "The Shop title and body copy could be two columns instead of one, simplify it". The same split header: "Shop" left, the pre-order line right, from 64rem.
15. "Item and price can be one line. Description font size could be increased a bit, kerning also increased". Name left and price right on one line (the price wraps under the name when there is no room). The description goes one size up (body size instead of small) with the body letter-spacing, same colour. "Coming soon" and all product text stay as they are.

## H. Not changed in round 5A
The home page, Our Story and the Initiative (5B covers the home hero, the photo pairs and Our Story), the menu's two columns, the shop grid, the colours.
