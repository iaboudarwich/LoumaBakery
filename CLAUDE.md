# Louma Bakery: Project Memory

You are building/maintaining the Louma Bakery website. Read this every session.

## What this is
A neighborhood bakery on Wilshire (Santa Monica), open and trading (as of September 2026), with an inclusive-employment mission (many teammates have intellectual or developmental differences; the bakery exists partly to show that done right). Founder: Milla Ghandour; named for her sister Louma. Tone: warm, plain-spoken, proud, never saccharine.

## Stack & rules
- Astro, static output, TypeScript strict. No heavy UI framework.
- All content lives in content collections / config (src/data/*.json), never hardcoded in markup. CMS-editable via Decap (self-hosted bundle in public/admin; /admin-demo is the zero-cred test-repo demo, config generated on prebuild).
- The header (Nav.astro, with the menu SiteMenu.astro) and the footer (Footer.astro) are single shared components used by every page; BaseLayout's `tone` prop (burgundy on the home page, butter elsewhere) sets the page, header and footer colours.
- Every decorative effect is flag-gated in src/config/features.ts, wired into /review, and respects prefers-reduced-motion + mobile. The header's compact transition and the menu fade are functional UI, not flagged, and are instant under reduced motion.
- HIDDEN FOR NOW (features.ts, all false): `cateringPage`, `careersPage`, `newsletter`. A hidden page is not built (the redirect in astro.config.mjs takes the route; the page also guards itself), 301s to / (netlify.toml + astro.config.mjs, via `hiddenRoutes`), is filtered from the sitemap. `newsletter` off = no footer signup anywhere. Keep every page file, form, script, data field and CMS field. Turning one back on: docs/HANDOFF.md section 4 (flip the flag AND delete its netlify.toml block).
- Production URL from one SITE_URL config value. Netlify hosting. The menu is edited in the CMS. The old Google Sheet sync (scripts/sync-menu.mjs) is guarded: it refuses to run without --force (the sheet is out of date and in the old shape). scripts/check-menu.mjs compares menu.json with the owner's list (brief E.14).
- Forms are Netlify Forms: data-netlify + data-netlify-honeypot + hidden form-name, posted via fetch by src/scripts/forms.ts (newsletter in the footer, careers, catering; all three hidden for now). forms.ts finds forms by the hidden form-name input (Netlify rewrites <form> tags at deploy). Form detection is on; email notifications are not set up yet (docs/HANDOFF.md).
- No prices anywhere on the site except shop products. Menu items have no price field; catering tier prices stay in copy.json but are not rendered.
- Every page's meta description comes from its own copy via describe() in src/lib/seo.ts; the default title/description is the owner's tagline (copy.json home).

## THE DESIGN SYSTEM ("editorial slab", the only layout)
Source of truth for the brand: docs/BRAND.md and docs/brand/ (the designer's board). Tokens: src/styles/tokens.css.
- PALETTE: exactly eight colours. --red #ED2739 (Louma Red), --burgundy #A10017, --peach #FF676C (the hover and focus accent), --tangerine #FF8926, --fairy #DDEAC3, --butter #FBF5D4, --white #FFFFFF, --ink #1D1D1B. Derived values (--body, --muted, --hairline-strong, the scrim) are color-mix() of these eight only. No other hex or rgb() anywhere in src/ (the theme-color meta and the inlined skip link carry the literal burgundy and butter).
- COLOUR ROLES (designer round 5B): burgundy and butter carry the site. The home page is burgundy with butter text and a butter footer; every other page is butter with burgundy text and a burgundy footer; the Menu and Shop bands are burgundy. .surface-burgundy / .surface-butter (global.css) set a block's background, text, link and focus colours. Red is for the page titles and section headings on butter pages (Story, Initiative, Contact, 404), at 24px and up (red on butter is 3.86:1). PEACH IS FOR HOVER AND FOCUS ONLY, never resting text: peach text fails on both surfaces (2.58:1 on butter, 2.93:1 on burgundy), so links keep their colour and the underline turns peach and 2px; icon controls (.icon-btn) keep their colour and show a 2px peach line under the icon; the primary button fills peach with ink text (5.96:1). Focus rings: 2px, burgundy on butter, butter on burgundy (7.56:1).
- FLAT: no box-shadows, no backdrop blur, no gradients, with one exception: the home hero's radial scrim behind the tagline (contrast, see /). Depth = surface change + 1px hairlines. The header has no rule under it.
- BANDS: every section is one committed block: butter, or a full-width block in burgundy, white or fairy (.block--*, .surface-*). The colour change is the divider. Louma Red is never a band.
- TYPE: the Owners superfamily (Ohno Type Co), woff2 subsets in public/fonts, declared in src/styles/fonts.css. Three faces: Owners Narrow 700 (--font-display) uppercase for every title, heading and label (VISIT, SAY HI, MORE, .label), the hero tagline, product names, the menu's category links and the menu links (tokens --display-weight 700 / --display-track 0.01em / --display-lh 1.05); Owners 400 for body, intros, menu items, notes, links, footer text and buttons; Owners 500 only for data and form fields (hours on /visit, inputs). "Follow along" on the home page is Owners 400, not uppercase. Exactly three files in public/fonts: owners-400, owners-500, owners-narrow-700. Preloads: owners-400, owners-narrow-700 (both above the fold on every page). BODY TRACKING: --track-body 0.01em on every text element (a zero-specificity :where() rule in global.css, so it resolves at each element's own size); headings keep --display-track. No italic ships and font-synthesis is off, so never ask for italics or other weights. Licence: the files are desktop-licensed; a webfont licence from Ohno Type Co is required before launch.
- TYPE SCALE (tokens.css; the designer's sizes at 1440, each scaling linearly to its phone size at 390): --fs-display 50px -> 32px (page titles, the hero tagline, "Follow along"); --fs-h2 35 -> 24 (section and menu category headings); --fs-lead 30 -> 20 (the home intro and visit row); --fs-body 25 -> 17 (body, intros, links, footer text, labels); --fs-item 25 -> 18 (menu items, product names and prices); --fs-small 20 -> 15 (menu notes, shop descriptions, buttons); --fs-fine 15 -> 13 (the copyright). Tight lines where the design is tight (menu items, notes, the bands, the footer: 1.15-1.2); paragraphs 1.4. text-wrap: pretty on paragraphs, balance on headings.
- BODY TEXT: no ink body copy. Page body paragraphs are burgundy at --fs-body (line-height 1.4, 1em apart): one shared rule in global.css (`.lead`, `.page-intro .lede`, `.text-row-body p`). The home intro sets --fs-lead in butter on top of it; the bands set butter.
- SHAPES: square. No border-radius anywhere: buttons, chips, inputs, cards, images, nav.
- TYPE FLOOR: 13px minimum for all rendered text.

Four columns from 48rem, tops level, in the container: the brand (the wordmark over the face, each clamp(76px,9.03vw,130px), static, side by side on phones); VISIT (the address on two lines, then each hours group from lib/hours.ts: "Tuesday – Sunday" / "7:30 AM – 2:30 PM", "Closed Mondays"); SAY HI (siteConfig.footer.sayHi links, empty for now, then the Instagram and TikTok icons: inline SVG 24px currentColor in 44px links named by aria-label, svg aria-hidden); MORE (siteConfig.footer.more: Contact Us -> /visit, Large Order Inquiries -> mailto the orders address). ## MOTION WHITELIST (complete; add nothing else)
- Doodle reveal (Doodle.astro, flag doodleDraw): one-shot 900ms cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1) when 30% of the drawing is in view. Every master is a filled outline, so it reveals left to right with a clip-path; stroked art would draw on via pathLength. Never left invisible (viewport watchdog). Drawings are accents, never background patterns; at most one per section (the letter's sun and moon are the specified exception).
- Sketch draw-on-scroll (flag scrollDraw; the 404 page's face only; the footer face is static): one-shot ~1.1s, 80ms subpath stagger; the outline traces in, then the fill settles back.
- Entrances: fade + settle 16px DOWNWARD (start 16px ABOVE) or fade in place; 500ms, same bezier, children staggered 80ms, once at ~30% in view. NEVER rise bottom-to-top.
- Photos never animate: no wipe, zoom, ken-burns, parallax or tilt. There is no marquee.
- Header: tall to compact in one height transition when the page scrolls (--dur, --ease-out). The menu: a --dur opacity fade in and out, and its roll draws itself once in 1.6s on each open (stroke-dashoffset on pathLength 1, flag doodleDraw). All instant or static with reduced motion. There is no looping animation on the site.
- Micro: button press scale, brand focus ring. Link underlines are plain solid lines (peach and 2px on hover and focus).
- Reduced motion: everything visible immediately in final state.

## COPY RULES (bans on sight)
- NO em dashes anywhere in rendered copy (verify dist HTML after building).
- No tricolons ("bread, pastry, and care"), no "not just X, it's Y", no empty superlatives (artisanal/finest/exceptional/premium), no jargon (elevate/journey/crafted/curated/nestled/indulge/savor/delight), no "we are passionate/committed/we believe", no throat-clearing openers.
- Text in story.json, copy.json and shop.json was written by the owner. Never rewrite, shorten or reword it, even when it uses words this guide otherwise avoids. Typos may be fixed only when asked.
- Caps: hero = one line (3-8 words) + one sentence. Menu items are the owner's names, with an optional short note (the owner's words, e.g. "Almond and pecan"). Story <= 300 words. Careers = plain sentences; the inclusive mission stated ONCE as plain fact. Visit = scannable mono block + one human line.

## MOBILE / iOS RULES (locked)
- Viewport units: min-height 100vh fallback + 100svh pair. svh, never dvh. Hero-minus-header = calc(100svh - var(--nav-h)).
- Home hero: full-bleed, min(55.6vw,800px) tall from 48rem, 70svh (70vh fallback) on phones, the tagline on two to three lines over its scrim.
- Never width:100vw; html,body { overflow-x: clip } stays.
- Safe areas: viewport-fit=cover; pad CONTENT with env(safe-area-inset-*) via max(gutter, inset); band backgrounds reach true edges; nav + body offset grow by the top inset.
- Hover behaviors ONLY behind @media (hover:hover) and (pointer:fine); touch gets tap alternatives wherever hover carries meaning.
- Inputs >=16px computed (iOS zoom); inputmode/autocomplete set; never suppress user zoom; html.kb-open hides the fixed header bar while a field is focused.
- Tap targets >=44px; -webkit-tap-highlight-color transparent + touch-action manipulation on controls.
- NO live SVG filters anywhere. FaceDraw (404) renders face-baked.svg (scripts/bake-sketches.mjs; equal to the master); the footer renders face.svg static; max 2 simultaneous draws, will-change on animating strokes.
- Header: the tall header, then the compact bar fixed to the top once scrolled (60px + safe-area below 48rem, 72px above; --nav-h, which anchor jumps and scroll-margin use); never hidden on scroll.
- Reveals: one shared IO, fail-safe (hidden states only under JS-set classes; JS off = everything visible).
- Hero image <=150KB on mobile (AVIF, eager, fetchpriority=high; the phone crop is 2:3, 600-1200w). Fonts: the Owners woff2 subsets only (Latin, Latin-1, general punctuation, arrows); owners-400 and owners-narrow-700 preloaded.
- theme-color AND body background match the page: burgundy #A10017 on the home page, butter #FBF5D4 everywhere else (Safari tints from either).
- Map: no iframe (CSP frame-src 'none'). /visit shows the hand-drawn map (map-hand-drawn.svg, butter on burgundy) and a plain "Get directions" link built from settings.json (siteConfig.directionsUrl).
- No sticky bottom bar on phones: the newsletter lives in the footer (switched off for now).
